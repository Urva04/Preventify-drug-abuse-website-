What is Drug Abuse?

The excessive use of harmful, illicit drugs and alcohol which lead to the misusing of pharmaceutical drugs is called Drug Abuse. It is a problem which is increasing in our society, specifically in teens and mid age people which are the major part of society. Given the rate of excessive Drug abuse, precautionary steps have to be taken to prevent this cause. Not only does it affects one's health but it also affects the society, the person's relationship with their family and breaks down one's self completely.

So by this website we are "Empowering You to Stay Drug-Free and healthy"

Why would one Take drugs?

Statistics

Statistics related to drug abuse are not just figures on paper, they represent real lives impacted, communities affected, and the scale of a problem that requires urgent attention. So, here we are showing the statistics of different types of Drug abuse in India, America and all over the World.

Alcohol

Snorting

Oral Intake

Injections

India

16 crore people (14.6%) between the age of 10 and 75 years are current users of alcohol, and out of them, 5.2% are alcohol dependents.(by NISD)

America

According to the 2023 National Survey on Drug Use and Health (NSDUH), 28.9 million people ages 12 and older (10.2%) had Alcohol Usage Disorder (AUD) in the past year.

World

The data on global alcohol consumption in 2019 shows that an estimated 400 million people aged 15 years and older live with alcohol use disorders, and an estimated 209 million live with alcohol dependence.

India

Around 2.1% adults with approx. 19 million users in country snort opioids like Charas, Ganja etc. In this even 1.8% of children (10-17yo) use opioids. (Survey- NISD)

America

In America, 5.5 million people used cocaine in 2018, with increasing overdose deaths. Methamphetamine use impacts 1.8 million, and snorting prescription stimulants is rising, particularly among youth​(src:NCDAS)

World

Globally, around 21 million people are estimated to use cocaine, and over 30 million use amphetamines, including methamphetamine. Snorting is one of the common methods of consuming these substances.(src:Statista)

India

The 2018 National Survey on Extent and Pattern of Substance Use revealed that around approximately 66 million adults take sedatives orally, highlighting a widespread issue across the country. This intakes includes sedatives, opioids, alcohol etc...

America

In America for oral intake MDMA (Ecstasy) and LSD are often taken in pill or tablet form.Prevalence: In 2022, an estimated 12.6 million people aged 12 or older reported using illicit drugs like ecstasy or hallucinogens, which are often orally ingested.

World

275 million people globally used drugs in 2020, with a portion consuming them orally.150 million women globally use oral contraceptive pills as their primary birth control method too

India

1.1 million people in India are estimated to use injectable drugs, according to the 2020 United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) report. Injecting drug use is prevalent in urban areas, particularly among young adults.

America

High rates of injectable drug use are reported in urban areas such as: Philadelphia, San Francisco, New York City.

World

Approximately 11.8 million people worldwide are estimated to inject drugs (United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime, 2021).

Prevention

Here by Prevention we aren't pointing anyone out negatively but our goal is to focus on preventing the possible outcomes of unhealthy outcome after intaking substance. So consider these steps for a good start:

One should Identify their wants vs. needs

Do you need comfort or to cope with something that happened? Mental health issues and weak lifestyle may lead to intake and it's no wrong to relax but everything taken above limit is unwanted.

Get to the root of your desire to partake at the moment Is it boredom? enjoyment or to soothe stress? maybe even family conflict is one of the primary causes for substance use in young people, some even try it under peer pressure. But one should ask themselves that is this how I want to cope up with my problems?

Consider informing an accountable close one

Do you have someone who can give you a private signal if you’re about to partake more than what you intended to, perhaps? Then consider them as your partner for clean way. Sometimes asking for help isn't embarassing!

Start noting down your daily Limit of intake you might want to jot down the limit recommendations for your particular substance, or what your personal limit was the last time you participated, and keep that on your phone or somewhere visible to you.

Drug consumption

Cocaine

Marijuana

Alcohol

Opioids

Use

Dosage: 10–30 mg (inhaled/snorted).

Effects: Brief euphoria, increased alertness, and energy. Users may feel heightened confidence and sociability.

Misuse

Dosage: 30–100 mg (inhaled/snorted), potentially higher if smoked or injected.

Effects: Stronger and more intense feelings of euphoria, but with increased risk of paranoia, anxiety, and physical harm like high blood pressure and irregular heartbeat.

Addiction

Dosage: 100 mg or more (inhaled/snorted), or significantly higher doses if smoked (crack cocaine) or injected.

Effects: Users are unable to function normally without the drug also leads to risks of heart attack.

Use

Dosage: 10–30 mg of THC (the psychoactive compound in marijuana) per session.

Details: Occasional use where the individual might smoke or ingest a small amount of marijuana.It typically doesn’t lead to dependence when used infrequently.

Misuse

Dosage: 30–100 mg of THC per session.

Details: Regular use or higher amounts consumed in one sitting. This might involve daily use or using marijuana to cope with stress. Users may develop tolerance and need higher doses to achieve the same effects.

Addiction

Dosage: 100 mg of THC or more per session.

Details: Frequent and heavy use leading to dependence. Users may need large amounts of marijuana to feel normal and experience withdrawal symptoms like irritability, sleep disturbances, and cravings when not using the drug.

Use

Quantity: Up to 1-2 standard drinks per day for women and up to 2-3 standard drinks per day for men.

Details: This level of drinking is generally considered moderate and is often socially acceptable. It usually doesn’t lead to significant health problems or dependence if consumed responsibly.

Misuse

Binge Drinking: Consuming 4 or more standard drinks on a single occasion for women, and 5 or more for men.

Heavy Drinking: Consuming more than 7 standard drinks per week for women and more than 14 standard drinks per week for men.

Addiction

Quantity: There is no specific quantity that defines addiction, but it often involves drinking heavily on a regular basis, with a pattern of drinking:

Daily Consumption: Regularly consuming several standard drinks each day, such as 4 or more per day.

Use

Oxycodone: 5–15 mg per dose.

Hydrocodone: 5–10 mg per dose.

Heroin: 5–20 mg per use.

Misuse

Oxycodone: 20–80 mg per dose.

Hydrocodone: 15–30 mg per dose.

Heroin: 20–100 mg per use.

Addiction

Oxycodone: 80-160 mg per dose.

Hydrocodone: 30-60 mg per dose.

Heroin: 100-500 mg per use.
